Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you handle posting and commenting for clients?

We publish your content and comments using aged, established Reddit accounts with organic karma. This ensures your submissions conform with community guidelines and avoid aggressive spam filters.

When are my credits deducted?

Credits are only deducted once your post or comment is successfully verified live on the target subreddit. If a post is auto-filtered or removed by automod, credits are not charged.

Can I use my credits on other platforms?

Yes, your credit pool can be allocated across Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, and Medium placements, as well as our AI Subreddit Analysis and Customer Lead Mining tools.
